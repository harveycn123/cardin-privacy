简体中文

本政策说明 CardIn iOS 应用如何处理你的数据。CardIn 无需注册账户；名片文字识别在你的设备上完成。我们不运营接收名片内容的服务器，不出售个人数据，也不在应用中加入广告、跨应用跟踪或第三方分析 SDK。

1. 名片与本机处理

你拍摄、选择导入或手动输入的名片图片、识别文字、姓名、公司、职位、联系方式、备忘、时间和地点，用于识别、校对、保存、查找和整理名片。文字识别使用设备上的 Apple Vision 功能，图片和识别内容不会为 OCR 上传至我们的服务器或第三方 AI 服务。保存的数据存储在应用的本机数据空间；你可以选择是否保留名片图片。

2. iCloud 同步

应用默认尝试启用 iCloud 同步。你的设备已登录 Apple 账户且 iCloud 可用时，已保存的名片、图片、备忘等数据可通过 Apple CloudKit 同步至该账户的私有数据库及你的其他设备。你可以在 CardIn 设置中关闭同步。关闭同步会停止连接，但不会自动删除本机或 iCloud 中已有的数据。iCloud 由 Apple 提供，按 Apple 的隐私政策处理数据。

3. 系统权限与地图

  • 相机：用于你主动扫描或补拍名片。
  • 照片：通过系统照片选择器读取你选择导入的图片；将名片图片保存到相册时请求添加照片的权限。
  • 位置：仅在你选择“使用当前位置”并授权时获取位置，以填写交流地点；不进行持续后台定位。将坐标转换为地点名称时，会使用 Apple 地理编码服务。
  • 地图：使用地图功能时，相关地址或地点查询会发送给 Apple 地图／地理编码服务，以显示位置和提供导航。

你可以在 iOS 设置中管理相机、照片和位置权限。CardIn 不读取你的系统通讯录。

4. 购买、导出与联系

应用内购买由 Apple App Store 处理。CardIn 使用 StoreKit 检查购买权益并恢复购买，不接收你的银行卡信息。你主动导出或分享时，所选联系人、CSV 表格或名片图片会交给你选择的位置或应用；这些副本由你及接收方管理。如果你通过邮件联系我们，我们会使用你提供的邮箱、邮件内容和附件回复问题，不会要求你提供完整名片库。

5. 保留与删除

名片数据保留至你主动删除。你可以删除单张名片，或在设置中选择“删除全部数据”。启用 iCloud 同步时，删除会同步到 iCloud 和其他设备；关闭同步时，本机删除记录会在以后重新启用同步后传递。卸载应用会移除本机应用数据，但不会自动删除已有的 iCloud 副本、系统备份或已导出的文件。你可以通过 Apple 的 iCloud 储存空间和备份设置管理这些副本。我们没有可替你检索或删除名片内容的自有服务器。

6. 本网页与服务提供方

本政策页面由 GitHub Pages 托管，不添加分析脚本、广告或跟踪 Cookie。访问网页时，GitHub 可能为提供及保护托管服务处理 IP 地址等访问日志。Apple 服务与 GitHub 托管分别适用 Apple 隐私政策和 GitHub 隐私声明。

7. 更新与联系

功能或数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页和生效日期。隐私相关问题请联系 CardIn 开发者：support@kmate.io。

English

This policy explains how the CardIn iOS app handles your data. CardIn requires no app account, and business card text recognition runs on your device. We do not operate servers that receive your card content, sell personal data, or include advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs in the app.

1. Cards and on-device processing

Card images you capture or select, recognized text and information you enter—including names, companies, job titles, contact details, notes, dates and places—are used to recognize, review, save, find and organize cards. Text recognition uses Apple Vision on your device. Images and recognized content are not uploaded to our servers or third-party AI services for OCR. Saved data is kept in the app’s local storage. You can choose whether to keep card images.

2. iCloud sync

The app attempts to enable iCloud sync by default. When your device is signed in to an Apple Account and iCloud is available, saved cards, images, notes and related data can sync through Apple CloudKit to that account’s private database and your other devices. You can turn sync off in CardIn settings. Turning it off stops the connection but does not automatically delete existing local or iCloud data. Apple provides iCloud and handles its data under Apple’s privacy policy.

3. Permissions and maps

  • Camera: used when you choose to scan or photograph a card.
  • Photos: the system photo picker provides the images you select for import. Permission to add photos is requested when you save card images to your photo library.
  • Location: accessed only when you choose “Use Current Location” and grant permission, to fill in a meeting place. The app does not continuously track location in the background. Apple’s geocoding service is used to turn coordinates into a place name.
  • Maps: when you use map features, relevant address or place queries are sent to Apple’s mapping or geocoding services to show locations and provide navigation.

You can manage camera, photo and location permissions in iOS settings. CardIn does not read your system address book.

4. Purchases, exports and contacting us

Apple App Store processes in-app purchases. CardIn uses StoreKit to check purchase entitlements and restore purchases; we do not receive your payment card details. When you choose to export or share, the selected contacts, CSV files or card images are passed to the destination or app you select. You and the recipient manage those copies. If you email us, we use your email address, message and attachments to respond. We do not ask you to send your entire card library.

5. Retention and deletion

Card data is retained until you delete it. You can delete individual cards or use “Delete All Data” in settings. With iCloud sync enabled, deletions sync to iCloud and your other devices. If sync is off, local deletion records are passed on when sync is enabled again. Uninstalling the app removes its local data but does not automatically delete existing iCloud copies, system backups or exported files. You can manage those copies through Apple’s iCloud storage and backup settings. We have no developer-operated server from which we can retrieve or delete your card content for you.

6. This webpage and service providers

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages and includes no analytics scripts, ads or tracking cookies. GitHub may process access logs, such as IP addresses, to provide and secure its hosting service. Apple services and GitHub hosting are respectively covered by the Apple Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Updates and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when features or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the CardIn developer at support@kmate.io.
